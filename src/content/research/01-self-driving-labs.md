Decades of materials research have produced synthesis routes, characterization methods, and chemical intuition that no algorithm can replace. We build on this human expertise and add robotics and AI only where they genuinely accelerate the work.

Our robotic platforms are designed around the dexterity and experience of human researchers, so that automation reproduces proven experimental practice rather than reinventing it. We study AI-driven decision making—choosing the next experiment and learning from its result—and develop systematic ways to incorporate human domain knowledge into this learning loop, so that the model's decisions are refined by expert insight rather than by data alone.

Our goal is a laboratory in which human, AI, and robot each do what they do best, and together accelerate the development of materials and devices.
