In pursuing this, we build a self-driving lab that is both performance-driven and knowledge-aware. Our robust platform explores two design axes together. The first is materials-level design—defect engineering, porous structure, morphology and size, and crystalline structure—and the physico-chemical properties they give rise to. The second is device-level operating conditions—temperature, pressure, and the micro-environment around the active material—which decide the final performance in practice yet have rarely been treated as tunable variables in self-driving labs.

By generating end-to-end datasets that link synthesis, structure, properties, and device performance, the platform serves two goals at once. **Performance-driven:** optimize for high performance at scale, not only under fixed laboratory conditions. **Knowledge-aware:** uncover the underlying design principles that govern both axes, with the principles emerging in the loop rather than after it.
