Jiheon Kim received the Ph.D. in Chemical and Biological Engineering from Seoul National University with Prof. Taeghwan Hyeon, designing nanostructured electrocatalysts for energy conversion and storage. As a Postdoctoral Researcher with Prof. David Sinton and Prof. Edward H. Sargent at the University of Toronto, Jiheon integrated laboratory automation and AI/ML with prior expertise in materials synthesis and energy conversion to accelerate the discovery of energy materials. At KAIST, Jiheon aims to accelerate the development of materials and devices for decarbonization through autonomous laboratories.
